Symptoms That Mean It's Time to Call

Most tanks announce trouble weeks or months ahead with rumbling noises, rusty water, or hot showers that keep getting shorter. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Rusty water or damp spots around the equipment
  • Distant fixtures that take minutes to run warm
  • Loud sediment noise from equipment that runs all day
  • Dish or hand sink temperatures drifting below what your inspector expects
  • Unsteady delivery temperatures that suggest a failing mixing valve
  • Higher utility bills that point to a heater losing efficiency
  • An aging unit you would rather replace on your schedule than in an emergency
  • The tank giving up in the middle of a rush

Early Action Saves Money

Left alone, even minor symptoms like rumbling or temperatures that swing from hot to warm tend to mean the heater is working its way toward a breakdown. At the first hint of a problem, reach out to Brunswick Water Heater at (912) 205-6650 and we'll take a look.

Tips to Keep Your Water Heater Healthy

Most of the failures we see could have been delayed for years with basic upkeep. Put these technician-approved steps to work at home:

  • Listen for a failing recirculation pump and have check valves inspected
  • Leave space around commercial heaters for airflow and safe servicing
  • Check anodes on a schedule matched to your volume and water quality
  • Log water temperatures at key fixtures so drift is caught early
  • Schedule upgrades during slow periods instead of waiting for a breakdown
  • Test thermostatic mixing valves periodically to confirm safe delivery temperatures
  • Flush commercial tanks more often than residential ones, often every few months in heavy use or hard water
