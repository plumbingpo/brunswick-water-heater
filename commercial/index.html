Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing shows up first as lukewarm water at the dish machine and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temp dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater paired with the main supply. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, with service scheduled around your hours.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize during the rush. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we install overnight or before opening, so hot water is ready when the first shift arrives.

Code & Inspections

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect flue draft and vent connections, verify adequate combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Consistent professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

Each hour without hot water costs a business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the dish room. In a salon, it empties the chairs. For a hotel, it means refund requests, and in a multifamily building it invites tenant disputes. Brunswick Water Heater offers fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties across the area, and we can schedule planned work after hours to keep downtime short.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including failed gas valves, burned-out elements, flame sensor and ignition faults, high-limit lockouts, leaking tanks, recirculation pumps that stopped, and tankless error-code lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, valving off a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records.

Ongoing Protection

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Brunswick Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water quality, usage patterns, and budget.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses on a regular schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and steadier water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
